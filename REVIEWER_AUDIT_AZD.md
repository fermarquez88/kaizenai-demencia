# Second critical peer review (self-audit as an *Alzheimer's & Dementia* reviewer) and resolution

> A second audit from the perspective of a methodologically demanding *Alzheimer's & Dementia* reviewer, with the
> additional analyses this journal would require, and how the manuscript was revised.

## Major concerns

**AD-1 — Outcome not anchored to established criteria/biomarkers.** The severity-based outcome is not tied to
NIA-AA/ATN or to a biomarker-confirmed diagnosis. *Resolution:* the outcome is explicitly reframed as **progression of
severity** (moderate-or-greater impairment), and we add a **face-validity benchmark**: the observed **annualized
progression rate was 11.0%/year** (32 events / 290 person-years; crude 24% over 1.9 y), within the published
memory-clinic range of ~10–15%/year.[3] Biomarker anchoring (regional AT(N)) is flagged as the validation path.

**AD-2 — Incremental value over demographics not demonstrated.** Age alone is a strong predictor; the journal would ask
whether neuropsychology adds. *Resolution:* we add an **incremental-value analysis** (new Figure 5C): baseline delayed
memory increased AUC over **age alone** by **ΔAUC +0.13** (MCI→dementia: 0.72→0.85) and **+0.08** (pre-dementia:
0.69→0.78), establishing that the memory measure carries information beyond demographics.

**AD-3 — MCI reversion ignored.** A substantial fraction of MCI reverts; ignoring it biases prognosis. *Resolution:* we
now report **reversion (MCI→normal) in 6% (5/85)** and overall improvement of ≥1 band in 13%, consistent with the
literature, and discuss it as evidence against a deterministic view of impairment.

**AD-4 — Practice effects in serial testing.** *Resolution:* the reliable-change framework and our **locally derived
RCIs** partially account for expected retest change; absence of a formal practice-effect correction is acknowledged.

**AD-5 — Missing-data handling.** *Resolution:* quantified (age 0%, delayed memory 6%, baseline severity 0% in the
pre-dementia cohort) and handled by median imputation plus missingness indicators **inside** cross-validation.

**AD-6 — Competing risk of death and time-to-event structure.** *Resolution:* retained as an explicit limitation; a
discrete-time survival model with competing risks is stated as the next analytic step.

**AD-7 — NLP-derived outcome without expert adjudication.** *Resolution:* dual independent coding (severity κ=1.00) and
construct validity against the objective z-reference are emphasized; a clinician-adjudicated validation subset is
proposed as future work.

**AD-8 — Single site; no external validation; generalizability.** *Resolution:* reinforced; regional (ReDLat) external
validation prioritized.

## Enhancements to figures (per the request for higher-level scientific graphics, in Spanish)
- **Figura 1** — participant-flow diagram (STROBE/TRIPOD).
- **Figura 2** — severity **transition matrix** (baseline→final), the direct visual of *how profiles evolve*.
- **Figura 3** — phenotype decline **forest plot** with Wilson 95% CIs and omnibus test.
- **Figura 4** — **regression-to-the-mean** scatter (baseline z vs Δz), making the key methodological confound visible.
- **Figura 5** — model performance: **ROC curves**, **calibration plot**, and **incremental value** (A/B/C).
- **Figura 6** — deployed calculator.

## Net effect
The revision adds the analyses an *Alzheimer's & Dementia* reviewer would demand—**annualized progression benchmarking,
incremental value over age, reversion rates, missing-data accounting**—and upgrades the figures to a higher scientific
standard. Combined with the first (*Neurology*) audit, the work is now an honest, well-quantified **exploratory** study
with transparent limitations and open, reproducible materials.
